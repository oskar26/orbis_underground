# Orbis Underground — Credits

## Get Hy! by DaemonPalace (MIT License)
- Cannabis plant models (Sativa, Indica, Hybrid — 3 growth stages each)
- Joint, Blunt, Spliff, Cone, and Twax 3D models
- 5-tier quality system logic (Junk/Street/Dispensary/Exotic/God)
- Herb Bag decorative block model
- Eternal Seeds mechanic
- Seed Bag textures (base for new seed bag recolors)
Assets reused under the MIT license.

- PixExact (https://www.pixexact.com) for AI-generated pixel art
- Retro Diffusion (https://retrodiffusion.ai) for plant textures
